# TripCraft Mobile V127

- Based on verified Mobile V126.
- Sends authenticated user_id and stable trip_id to AI build/verify calls.
- Adds required arrival and departure flight times.
- Sends airports/times to AI for first/last-night planning.
- Day drill-down uses AI day summaries and activity descriptions.
- Adds per-stop Google Maps and Waze links.
- Preserves V126 behavior and bilingual pages.
