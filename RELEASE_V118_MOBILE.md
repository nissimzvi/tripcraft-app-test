# TripCraft Mobile V118

V118 is based only on the approved V117 release.

## Corrections

- The language control now always shows `EN` in Hebrew mode and `HE` in English mode.
- Hebrew and English direction and labels are restored consistently after navigation and refresh.
- The planner `Next` action is captured reliably on iPhone and keeps clear required-field validation.
- The planner `Back` action returns through dates, flights, and destination without deleting entered data.
- From the first destination screen, `Back` returns a signed-in user to My Account and a guest to Home.
- The last planner step continues to show only `Back` and `Build Trip`.
- Provence is recognized from `פרובאנס`, `פרובנס`, `Provence`, and `Provence, France`, including manual input.
- Provence planning continues to use Avignon, Aix-en-Provence, and Luberon lodging hubs.

## Preserved from V117

Account trips, save/update and discard flows, AI change requests, day details,
kosher lodging verification, hotel changes, mobile layout, dates, and personal links.
